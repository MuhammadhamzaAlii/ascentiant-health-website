Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Ascentiant Health (“Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide through our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you voluntarily submit it through our website forms:

  • Name
  • Organization name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Specialty and general business information
Please do not submit protected health information (PHI) through our website forms. Our contact forms are not intended for the transmission of patient-level medical data.

2. How We Use Information

We use submitted information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Schedule consultations
  • Provide requested information about our services
  • Improve our website and communications

3. Data Security

Oversight and governance of our operations are directed from our headquarters in San Diego, California. We maintain structured operational controls, role-based access limitations, and documented procedures designed to safeguard information and support regulatory compliance standards.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers (such as secure form processing platforms and hosting providers) to operate our website. These providers are contractually obligated to maintain appropriate data protections.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers solely for operational purposes related to responding to your inquiry.

6. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to request information regarding personal data collected and the right to request deletion, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

7. Data Retention

We retain submitted information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above or as required by law.
