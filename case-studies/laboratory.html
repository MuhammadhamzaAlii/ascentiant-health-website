Laboratory

Independent Diagnostic Laboratory

An independent lab processing high-volume diagnostic claims faced persistent underpayments, payer contract variance, and test code bundling issues — with limited reporting to pinpoint where revenue was leaking.

Anonymized engagement summary. Client identity withheld. Results represent outcomes from a representative engagement; individual results may vary.
The Challenge

Underpayments, Contract Variance, and Bundling Complexity

High claim volume masked significant revenue leakage. The lab's prior billing operation lacked the payer contract analytics and code-level scrutiny needed to catch underpayments and bundling errors at scale.

Systematic Underpayments

Payers were reimbursing below contracted rates on a meaningful share of claims. Without automated contract comparison, underpayments accumulated silently across commercial and government payers.

Payer Contract Variance

Dozens of active payer contracts with different fee schedules, panel definitions, and modifier rules made manual reconciliation impractical and error-prone.

Test Code Bundling Errors

Incorrect bundling and unbundling of CPT codes triggered denials and underpayments — particularly on multi-test panels and molecular diagnostics where payer edit rules differed significantly.

Reporting Blind Spots

Leadership received aggregate collection reports but couldn't drill into underpayment trends by payer, test category, or referring provider — limiting their ability to act on data.

The Solution

Expert RCM + Platform Visibility

Ascentiant deployed a dedicated laboratory revenue cycle management team and configured platform reporting to surface underpayments, contract variances, and bundling issues in real time.

Revenue Cycle Management

  • Contract rate validation against expected reimbursement on every claim
  • Underpayment identification, appeal, and recovery workflows
  • CPT bundling and unbundling review for panel and molecular tests
  • Payer-specific edit rule management and denial prevention
  • High-volume claim scrubbing with lab-specific compliance checks

Revenue Cycle Operations Platform

  • Underpayment dashboards by payer, test code, and time period
  • Contract variance alerts when reimbursement falls below expected
  • Clean claim rate monitoring with edit failure categorization
  • Test category performance reporting for lab leadership
  • Executive visibility into recovery progress and denial trends
Results

Recovered Revenue and Full Reporting Control

Within the first 90 days, the lab recovered significant underpaid balances and achieved a clean claim rate approaching industry best-in-class levels.
