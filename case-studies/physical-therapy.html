Physical Therapy

Multi-Location Physical Therapy Group

A growing PT practice with multiple clinic locations faced rising denial rates from KX modifier errors and visit cap issues — until Ascentiant Health tightened billing compliance and accelerated collections.

Anonymized engagement summary. Client identity withheld. Results represent outcomes from a representative engagement; individual results may vary.
The Challenge

Modifier Errors and Visit Cap Denials Were Eroding Collections

This multi-location physical therapy group was expanding rapidly, but billing complexity was outpacing internal capacity. KX modifier application errors and visit cap denials were the top revenue leakage points, slowing collections across all locations.

KX Modifier Errors

Inconsistent application of the KX modifier for medically necessary services beyond therapy caps caused frequent rejections and rework — especially when plans of care crossed calendar-year thresholds.

Visit Cap Denials

Medicare and commercial payers enforced visit limits with varying rules. Without centralized tracking, therapists and front desk staff couldn't reliably flag cap proximity before claims went out.

First-Pass Acceptance Gaps

First-pass acceptance had stalled at 91%, with edit failures tied to modifier sequencing, units of service, and plan-of-care date misalignment across locations.

Slow Collection Velocity

Denial-driven rework and inconsistent follow-up extended the collection cycle, creating cash flow pressure as the group opened additional clinic sites.

The Solution

Expert RCM + Platform Visibility

Ascentiant assigned therapy-specialized billers and deployed physical therapy revenue cycle management workflows to standardize modifier compliance, visit cap monitoring, and denial prevention across every location.

Revenue Cycle Management

  • KX modifier validation and plan-of-care compliance review
  • Visit cap tracking with proactive alerts before threshold breach
  • Pre-submission scrubbing for modifier sequencing and units
  • Denial root-cause analysis and structured appeals workflow
  • Centralized A/R follow-up across all clinic locations

Revenue Cycle Operations Platform

  • Location-level claim work queues and productivity tracking
  • Visit cap dashboards by patient, payer, and clinic
  • Denial trend reporting by reason code and therapist
  • First-pass acceptance monitoring with edit failure drill-down
  • Executive dashboards on net collections and A/R aging
Results

Cleaner Claims and Faster Cash Across All Locations

Within the first 90 days, the group achieved near-industry-leading first-pass rates and a significant reduction in therapy-specific denials.
