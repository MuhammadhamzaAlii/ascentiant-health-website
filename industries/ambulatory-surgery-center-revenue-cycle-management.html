Industry Challenges

Revenue Cycle Challenges Facing Ambulatory Surgery Centers

Ambulatory Surgery Centers face some of healthcare's most complex reimbursement requirements.

Every surgical procedure involves multiple operational and financial processes including scheduling, insurance verification, prior authorizations, coding, documentation, claims submission, payment posting, and ongoing payer follow-up.

Common challenges include:

As procedure volume grows, disconnected workflows and manual reporting often make it difficult to identify where revenue is delayed or lost.

  • Prior authorization management
  • Procedure coding accuracy
  • Documentation deficiencies
  • Commercial payer requirements
  • Medicare reimbursement
  • Claim denials
  • Underpayments
  • Aging Accounts Receivable
  • Credentialing delays
  • Multiple physician schedules
  • Limited operational visibility
