  • Prior authorization management
  • Authorization renewals

  • Payer-specific reimbursement rules
  • Modifier & revenue optimization

Our goal isn't simply to submit claims. Our goal is to maximize appropriate reimbursement while maintaining compliance and reducing administrative burden.

Operational Expertise

Proactive Authorization & Benefit Management

PT revenue cycle success requires continuous monitoring—not reactive follow-up after denials arrive.

  • Continuous authorization monitoring
  • Tracking remaining visits
  • Monitoring benefit maximums
  • Applying for authorization renewals before visits expire
  • Preventing denials instead of reacting to them

Many Physical Therapy practices lose revenue because authorization renewals are not obtained before approved visits or payer benefit maximums are exhausted.

Our team proactively monitors these throughout treatment to help prevent avoidable denials before they occur—one of the most common causes of lost revenue in Physical Therapy.
