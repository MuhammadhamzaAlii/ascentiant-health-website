Revenue Cycle Management Built for Clinical Laboratories

Clinical laboratories process thousands of tests, multiple payer types, and highly specialized reimbursement rules every day. Even small breakdowns between laboratory operations and billing can result in delayed reimbursement, denied claims, and lost revenue. Ascentiant Health provides technology-enabled Revenue Cycle Management designed specifically for laboratories, combining experienced billing professionals with our Revenue Cycle Operations Platform to improve collections, strengthen operational visibility, and reduce revenue leakage.

Industry Challenges

Revenue Cycle Challenges Facing Clinical Laboratories

Laboratories operate in one of healthcare's highest-volume billing environments. Every day, test orders, specimen processing, payer requirements, coding rules, and reimbursement guidelines must align to ensure accurate claims and timely payment.

Common challenges include:

Many laboratories have excellent billing systems but lack the operational reporting needed to identify where revenue is delayed or lost.

  • High claim volume
  • Complex payer requirements
  • Medical necessity documentation
  • Prior authorization requirements
  • Coding accuracy
  • Claim denials
  • Aging Accounts Receivable
  • Underpayments
  • Laboratory order reconciliation
  • Revenue leakage
  • Limited operational visibility

Reconciliation

Laboratory Order Reconciliation

One of the greatest opportunities within laboratory Revenue Cycle Management is identifying revenue that never reaches the billing process.

Future versions of our Revenue Cycle Operations Platform are designed to help laboratories reconcile completed laboratory activity against billed claims.

By comparing operational laboratory data with billing information, organizations can identify:

This type of operational reconciliation provides visibility that traditional billing systems often do not deliver.

  • Completed tests that were never billed
  • Documentation deficiencies
  • Missing claims
  • Delayed billing
  • Revenue leakage
  • Workflow bottlenecks
