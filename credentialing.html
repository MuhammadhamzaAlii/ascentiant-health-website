Our Process

Structured Timeline from Application to Enrollment

A disciplined, trackable process that keeps credentialing moving — not stuck in payer queues.

1

Intake & Documentation

Collect provider credentials, licenses, malpractice insurance, work history, and practice information. Verify completeness before submission.

2

CAQH & Profile Setup

Create or update CAQH ProView profiles, attest provider data, and prepare standardized credentialing packets for payer submission.

3

Payer Applications

Submit enrollment applications to targeted payers, track submission status, and respond to payer requests for additional documentation.

4

Follow-Up & Escalation

Persistent payer follow-up, status monitoring, and escalation for stalled applications. Average enrollment timelines vary by payer — we keep them moving.

5

Contracting & Fee Schedules

Review executed contracts, confirm fee schedules, and validate effective dates so billing begins at the correct contracted rates.

6

Ongoing Maintenance

Continuous monitoring of revalidation deadlines, license renewals, and profile updates. Credentialing is never "done" — we keep it current.

Why It Matters

Credentialing Gaps Are Revenue Gaps

The Cost of Delays

Every day a provider isn't enrolled with a payer is a day of lost revenue. New providers, new locations, and new payer contracts all require timely enrollment — and most organizations underestimate the complexity.

Prevention Over Recovery

Claims submitted before enrollment is complete are denied — and recovering that revenue takes weeks. Ascentiant's integrated approach ensures enrollment is confirmed before billing begins.
