Transition & Onboarding

How We Transition

Switching billing partners sounds stressful. It doesn't have to be. Here's exactly what happens — timeline, handoff, training, and who does what.

How long?

Usually 2–6 weeks. Simpler setups can move faster. Complex multi-location groups may take a bit longer — we'll tell you upfront.

Will billing stop?

No. We run parallel workflows during handoff. Claims keep going out. Cash keeps coming in.

How painful?

Less than you'd think. We do the heavy lifting — data pull, A/R reconciliation, workflow mapping. Your team gets a clear checklist, not chaos.

Who trains us?

Your dedicated onboarding lead. Live walkthroughs of the platform, reporting, and escalation paths — not a 40-page manual and "good luck."

The Process

Five Steps. No Surprises.

Every transition follows the same structured playbook — adjusted for your size, specialty, and current vendor situation.

  1. Discovery & Fit Call

    30 minutes. We learn your EHR, payers, volumes, and pain points. You learn if we're a fit. No pressure.

  2. Data & Access Setup

    Secure EHR access, aging reports, denial exports, payer enrollment status. We send you a simple intake checklist.

  3. Parallel Run

    We work alongside your current process (or pick up open items) while mapping workflows. Billing continuity is the rule — not the exception.

  4. Go-Live & Handoff

    Cutover on an agreed date. First claims under our team. A/R reconciled. Denial queues prioritized. Platform access turned on for your leadership.

  5. Training & First 90 Days

    Platform training for your team. Weekly performance check-ins early on. Then a steady reporting cadence once things stabilize.
